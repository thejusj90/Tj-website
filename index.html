The trilogy

Three seats.
Three perspectives.

The roles were different by design: first building as a founder, then operating startup infrastructure at scale, and later building a sector-focused ecosystem. Each seat changed how I think about what founders actually need.

01

The Founder

Founded Techjeeva at 21 in Kerala. Built in embedded systems, IoT, drones and student technology communities — and experienced ecosystem gaps firsthand.

What that seat taught me:
Founders experience ecosystems as access — to customers, capital, talent, credibility and people willing to take a bet on them.
02

The Program Builder

At T-Hub Hyderabad, led Lab32 and worked across startup selection, investor access, corporate innovation, founder communities and operating scale.

What that seat taught me:
Great ecosystems are operational systems. Selection, capital, mentors, corporate access and community have to work together.
03

The Category Builder

At FiiRE Goa, built the TourismTech vertical, led funding and accelerator programs, and later led the institution as CEO through March 2026.

What that seat taught me:
A sector ecosystem becomes useful when startups, industry, government, capital and market access stop operating as separate silos.
